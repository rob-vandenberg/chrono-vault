# D-027: categories and templates

**Taken in autonomous mode** (G-26) on 22 November 2026, within the owner's changelist for `ROADMAP.md` steps 6 to 9
(Q19 taken as recommended; the owner: "when in doubt, do it as the reference program does").

## What was decided

1. **Items are made from templates, and every template belongs to a category**, as in the reference program.
2. **Eight built-in categories with thirteen templates**:
   1. Login: Login, Email account
   2. Credit card: Credit card
   3. Identity: Identity, Passport, Driver's license
   4. Secure note: Secure note
   5. Password: Password
   6. Finance: Bank account
   7. Computer: Wi-Fi, Server, Software license
   8. Miscellaneous: Membership

   Every built-in template ends with Notes. Their names and their fields' names are translated (D-008); US English
   spelling in the program.
3. **The person's own categories and templates are kept inside the vault**, sealed with it, so they travel with it.
   A category of their own can be renamed, and deleted only when no item or template uses it. A template of their
   own can be made from nothing or as a copy of any template, changed (name, category, and fields: name, kind,
   order) and deleted. Built-in ones are never changed or deleted (ref).
4. **Any category can be hidden from the left column** (ref); a hidden category no longer narrows the list.
5. **An item keeps its own fields**: changing or deleting a template never changes an item made from it.
6. **Settings > Customize** holds the categories and templates of the open vault. They are kept in the vault at once,
   not with the settings window's OK; Restore defaults leaves them alone.
7. **"New Item" offers every category shown in the left column**, each with its templates. The item editor moves
   fields up and down, removes any field, and adds a field of several lines (`REQUIREMENTS.md` 4.4).
8. **An item without an icon of its own shows its category's icon**; the person's own categories show the
   Miscellaneous icon.
9. **The contents format 3** (D-015) holds the template and category of every item, the person's own categories
   and templates, and the hidden categories. Formats 1 and 2 are still read: their Login and Secure note become the
   built-in Login and Secure note.

**Since 0.6.88 (D-066) Settings has no Customize and no Clipboard: Categories, Tags and Templates are topics of their
own, the rest is under General.**
