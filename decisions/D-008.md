# D-008: translations from the start

Approved by the owner on 21 November 2026, before any code existed.

## What was decided

1. **Chrono Vault speaks more than one language, and this is built in from the start**, not added later.
2. **US English is the primary language.** Every new text is written in US English; other languages are translations
   of it.
3. **Translations are made with Poedit.**

## Why

Texts added without translation in mind have to be found and reworked one by one later. Built in from the first
window, every text goes through the translation call from the day it is written.
