# D-001: the five platforms

Approved by the owner on 20 November 2026, before any code existed.

## What was decided

**Chrono Vault runs on Windows, Linux, macOS, Android and iOS.** On Linux: the Debian family and the Red Hat family,
under X11 and under Wayland (`REQUIREMENTS.md` chapter 2).

## Why

A password manager is used wherever the person logs in: on the computer and on the phone. A vault that cannot be
opened on one of their devices is a vault they cannot rely on.

## What it costs

A build, a package and a signing step for each platform, and store review for Android and iOS. The owner's own
testing covers five platforms.
