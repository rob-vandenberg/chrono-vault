# D-067: The background of every pop-up

Approved by the owner on 25 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.6.90).

1. Every menu, submenu and choice box's list stands on the main menu's background: the buttons' colour, a step lighter
   than the sidebar and the window in the dark theme and a step darker in the light one.
2. It is set once, in our style (`style/Menu.qml`, `style/ComboBox.qml`, `style/PopupPanel.qml`), so a pop-up made later
   has it too; the unlock page's list of vaults, which has a frame of its own, takes the same colour.
3. A menu takes its colours from what it belongs to, so it follows a change of theme while Chrono Vault runs; one made
   inside a row of a list did not.
4. Text fields have no right-click menu of their own in this Qt.
