# D-025: the main window's layout, after the reference program

Approved by the owner on 22 November 2026 (changelist of version 0.0.9), after the owner's picture of the reference
program's main window: its general layout, not each of its options; what Chrono Vault does not have yet is left out.

## What was decided

1. **One bar at the top.** On the left the menus: **File** (New vault…, Open vault…, Lock, Preferences…, Quit),
   **View** (Light, Dark, Follow the system and UI scaling…, each opening the preferences at General), **Item** (New
   login, New secure note, Delete item…), **Tools** (Generate password…) and **Help** (About Chrono Vault, F1; Check
   for update). On the right, on the same line: a filled **+ New Item** (Login or Secure note), a divider, **Generate
   Password** with an icon, and the lock and the preferences as icon buttons. "Preferences…" is the owner's word for
   the settings in the File menu. New vault and Open vault work from the start page, with no vault unlocked, as
   before; what needs an unlocked vault is greyed until then. The generator on its own copies its password.
2. **Three columns below it.** Left: the vault (its icon, its name, "Local file") and Home. Middle: the search, "Sort
   By: Title", and the items under letter headings (# for a title not starting with a letter), each with its icon,
   its title in bold and a second line: the user name, or else the web address. Right: the chosen item read-only:
   its icon and title with the pencil that opens the editor in the same column, then its fields in a framed card,
   the name right-aligned in grey and the value beside it, web addresses as links, a "…" menu at the end of each row
   (Copy; Show or Hide on a password), the one-time code with a ring counting down its seconds.
