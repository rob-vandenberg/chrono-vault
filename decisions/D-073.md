# D-073: One standard dialog for a master password, a PIN or a finger

Approved by the owner on 25 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.6.103).

1. **One dialog, written once** (`qml/AskWindow.qml`), with a password face and a PIN face, in the look of the start-up page:
   the heading, the vault's large field, the large password field with the eye, and the buttons at the bottom right; 534 by 271
   inside at first, times the UI scaling. The start-up page stays as it is and shares its fields (`qml/VaultBox.qml`,
   `qml/BigPasswordField.qml`).
2. **Used for**: Open, Import… and Export… in Settings > Vaults; the vault of Sync Conflicts; the WebDAV server's password;
   Export; Import from another vault. Not for changing a master password, a new vault, setting a PIN or connecting to WebDAV.
3. **It shows**, when it applies, the waiting time after wrong passwords (counted for the vault asked for, D-041; in Export
   too), the keyfile line (D-031) and the error, and stays open after a wrong password.
4. **The list of vaults with padlocks** only in Settings > Vaults. Another vault picked empties the field; one that needs no
   password goes on at once.
5. **The PIN face** in Settings > Vaults, when the vault asked for has a usable PIN, with "Use master password instead".
6. **The finger or Windows Hello** is asked when the dialog opens, where it is on for that vault and the master password is
   not due; it is enough for Export (the owner). Not for Sync Conflicts (another master password) nor the server's password.
7. **The vault that is open stays open** until the other one was proven and is opened with it; Cancel changes nothing.

## How it is kept

`src/ui/vault_controller_ask.cpp` proves a password, PIN or finger for any vault, off the window's thread, and keeps the key
only while the dialog is open; `openProven`, `exportProven` and `importProven` use it.

**Since 0.6.105 (D-074): the dialog has "Remember my credentials".**
