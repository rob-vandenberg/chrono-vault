# D-003: the windows in Qt Quick

Approved by the owner on 21 November 2026, before any code existed.

## What was decided

**The windows are built with Qt Quick (QML) on all five platforms**: one window design, for touch and mouse alike.

## Why

One design to build and keep up instead of two, and one that works with a finger on a phone.

## What was refused

1. **Qt Widgets on computers and QML on phones**: two window designs to build and keep the same.
2. **Qt Widgets everywhere**: it handles touch poorly on a phone.
