# D-080: Cloud storage, and Google Drive

Approved by the owner on 26 November 2026 (Proceed, in autonomous mode, on the changelist of versions 0.8.114 and
0.8.115; Q1 to Q15 of that brainstorm).

## The design (the owner's)

1. **A base class "cloud storage" with a class derived from it per storage**: WebDAV now, Google Drive next, later
   pCloud, and maybe OneDrive and Dropbox. The design is generic for every one of them, never specific to WebDAV and
   Google Drive.
2. **The sync and merge stay one class**, `CloudSync`, that works with any cloud storage: the working copy, the base
   file, the merge (D-054), the schedule, the conflicts window, the "Not synchronized" line.
3. **A storage that is not available in a build is not shown at all**, anywhere (the owner, Q6).
4. **Google Drive comes before pCloud** (the owner, Q1): `ROADMAP.md` step 14.

## How it is built (0.8.114)

5. `src/ui/cloud_storage.h`: `CloudStorage`, with what every storage does: sign in, list its folder, download (unless
   unchanged), upload, delete, make a folder, a file's version tag, put an uploaded file in place of another, and a
   short lock where the storage has locks. "Replace only if unchanged" is the sync's own sequence over these: lock
   (a storage without locks says so), check the version tag right before, then put the new version in place.
6. Every answer is a `CloudReply` with one `CloudError` for every storage, so neither the sync nor a window asks which
   storage it was.
7. `CloudPlace`: where one vault is kept and how far this device got with it, stored per working copy under
   `vaults/<kind>` in `chrono-vault.conf`; the sign-in's secret in the system's store under `<kind>-<id>`. A WebDAV
   vault is stored exactly as before (`vaults/webdav`, the same keys, `webdav-<id>`): nothing to convert, nothing
   lost (OR-15).
8. `WebDavStorage` is derived from `CloudStorage` and makes every request with the WebDAV client of D-057, so WebDAV
   behaves exactly as before.
9. `cloudKinds()` is the one list of storages this build has (kind, name, the tile's picture, the places picture). It
   drives the "Storage locations" tiles of Open, Create and Move a vault, the line of each vault in Settings > Vaults
   and in Open a vault's list ("WebDAV: …"), the places picture in every list and dropdown (D-079 point 6), the title
   of Disconnect ("Disconnect from WebDAV"), Tools > Sync Now, and the line under the vault's name.
10. `tests/unit/test_cloud_sync.cpp` runs `CloudSync` with a storage of the test's own, without locks, and stops it at
    every step: nothing of either side is lost (OR-15.4).

## The other program's way (OR-16, read 26 November 2026)

11. It is chosen in its sync settings, signs in through the browser, keeps its vault in a hidden folder of its own on
    Drive, and leaves the vault on Drive when sync is turned off. Chrono Vault follows it where it fits the owner's
    decisions: sign-in through the browser, and nothing deleted on Drive unless the person ticks it (point 13 of the
    changelist). Where it differs, the owner's decision stands: a "Chrono Vault" folder the person can see in My Drive
    (Q14), and the permission `drive.file`, which sees only the vaults Chrono Vault made or was given (Q15).

## Google Drive (0.8.115)

12. **Only the permission `drive.file`** (the owner): Chrono Vault sees only what it made or was given. Google's
    pages (26 November 2026): such a permission needs no verification by Google.
13. **Sign-in**: Sign in with Google opens the browser, the person signs in at Google, and the answer comes back to
    Chrono Vault on this computer (Qt Network Authorization, the owner's Q4). The lasting sign-in (Google's refresh
    token) is kept in the system's store as a WebDAV password is, per vault; Google's sign-in taken away or not on
    this device asks "Sign in to Google Drive" again, with a button that opens the browser.
14. **The "Chrono Vault" folder** at the top of My Drive, made by Chrono Vault (the owner, Q14): new vaults go there,
    the file dialog starts there, New folder… works inside it. A vault Chrono Vault did not make is not shown; the
    window says "Only vaults created or moved here by Chrono Vault are shown. To bring in another vault, open it from
    this computer and use Move to Google Drive…" (the owner, Q15).
15. **Replace only if unchanged, Drive's way** (the owner, Q2: A). Google's documentation offers no write that is
    refused when the file changed meanwhile (checked 27 November 2026). So: the version tag is checked right before
    the write; every vault written keeps its version ("Keep Forever", which Drive allows for 200 versions of a file);
    right after the write, Drive's version history is read, and when the version just before ours is not the one
    checked, another device's version came in between: it is put back on top and merged in a new round, so nothing is
    lost (OR-15.3). Chrono Vault deletes its own kept versions beyond the newest 10. A version downloaded is used only
    when its checksum is that version's.
16. **Disconnect…** of a Google Drive vault has the tick box "Also delete the vault on Google Drive", off unless
    ticked; ticked, the vault goes to Drive's trash, where the person can still get it back. A WebDAV vault's
    Disconnect deletes nothing, as before (D-060), and shows no such box.
17. **Where it is said**: a Google Drive tile under Storage locations, "Move to Google Drive" through Settings >
    Vaults > Move…, "Google Drive: Chrono Vault/Home.chronovault" in the vault lines, "Google Drive" under the vault's
    name, and the owner's own pictures `art/storage-googledrive.svg` and `art/places-googledrive.svg`.
18. **The program's Google client** lives only in the project's private folder; the build scripts give it to the
    build, which writes it into the build folder, never into git (the owner, Q5). A build without it has no Google
    Drive (Q6). The push refuses it in any file meant for GitHub.
19. **Tests**: `tests/google_drive_test_server.*` is a Google of our own (sign-in, Drive's files, folders, versions
    and trash); `test_google_drive` covers sign-in through the browser, two devices, a version that came in at the
    same moment, a sign-in that ran out, access taken away, no network, the versions kept, Disconnect with delete,
    Move to Google Drive and the tile only with the client; `test_google_drive_interruptions` stops a sync at every
    step (OR-15.4).
