# D-005: where the vault is stored

First approved by the owner on 20 November 2026; **replaced by the owner on 21 November 2026** with the wording below.

## What was decided

1. **Four kinds of storage are required**, and they are built in this order:
   1. a local vault file, on the device's own disk or on a network share;
   2. WebDAV;
   3. pCloud;
   4. Google Drive.
2. **The technique for each is decided when development reaches it**, not now.
3. **Changes made on two devices are merged**, and what cannot be merged is shown to the person to settle (OR-15).

## Creating a vault

Added by the owner on 21 November 2026: storage works as in the reference program.

1. When the person creates a new vault, they choose where it is stored: this device only, or one of the storages above.
2. Chrono Vault creates the vault on that target and syncs with it from then on.
3. The device keeps its own encrypted working copy, synced with the target, so the vault also works offline.
4. A vault on this device only can be moved to one of the other storages later.

## What it replaced

The first wording stored the vault only as a file in a folder on computers and through the system's file picker on
phones, and refused code for each provider's own interface. The owner wants the three cloud storages supported as
such; how is open until each is reached.

## Known before the technique is chosen

1. Google recommends its `drive.file` permission with the Google Picker, which lets the person choose the files the
   program may use. The full-Drive permission needs Google's verification and a yearly paid security assessment.
2. pCloud offers WebDAV, but users of other programs report it as unreliable.
3. WebDAV in general also reaches Nextcloud and other WebDAV servers.
