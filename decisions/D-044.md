# D-044: --debug, and the identifier chrono-vault for the settings

Approved by the owner on 22 November 2026 (Proceed); point 3 removed for version 0.0.38 (D-026).

1. `--debug` on the command line: only then is the fingerprint log written, to `~/.local/share/chrono-vault/fingerprint.log`; the owner runs with it as standard, end users do not.
2. The organization and application name are `chrono-vault` (the identifier, AGENTS.md 2); the two files Chrono Vault keeps are named after it (D-026).
3. Removed in 0.0.38: the settings kept under "Chrono Vault" before 0.0.30 are no longer carried over (no backwards compatibility, D-026).
