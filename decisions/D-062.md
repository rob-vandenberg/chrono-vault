# D-062: What a person guesses is what happens

Approved by the owner on 24 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.5.82), from his test of 0.5.81 and
the report of an agent that used the program as a person would. His measure: if the person has never read the
documentation and has to guess how it works, whatever he guesses is how the software should work.

**Since 0.5.83 (D-063)** point 3 is changed: no "5 items selected" page; the focus stays where it is.

## What was decided

1. **The item list**: a click gives it the keyboard; every key scrolls to the item it chooses; letters jump to an item;
   Tab reaches it; a double click or Enter opens the editor; a right click has a menu.
2. **Delete and Backspace ask** "Move … to the Trash?" (Enter is Yes, Esc is No), everywhere an item goes to the Trash;
   in the Trash they ask about deleting permanently, where No has the keyboard. Then the next item is chosen and the
   line at the bottom says what was done. This changes D-033 (the Trash without a question) and D-039 (Backspace).
3. **Several items**: Ctrl+click, Shift+click, Shift with the keys, Ctrl+A; "5 items selected" at the right; Trash,
   Restore and Archive act on all after one question, Favorite at once. The Trash has Restore all and Empty Trash….
4. **Search**: Ctrl+F; Down and Enter go to the list; Esc clears it. An item not in the results is not shown at the
   right, and comes back when the search is gone.
5. **The sidebar** takes the keyboard on a click; its keys choose a line and show its items.
6. **The item**: a double click on a value copies it, a right click on a field opens its menu. **The editor** opens on
   its title; Esc cancels (asked when changed; changes D-048), Ctrl+S saves.
7. **Dialogs**: Esc closes the one that has the keyboard, also one made inside another; a question opens with its
   button holding the keyboard, and Enter presses it. What cannot be undone never has Enter.
8. **Sync conflicts**: every key of a list, Ctrl+A, ranges with Shift; Enter applies; the normal scroll bar (D-049).
9. **Settings > Vaults**: Create vault and Open vault at the section's left edge, no Restore defaults (changes D-022);
   a gold crown after the primary vault's name.
10. **Every button** has 5 pixels free past its edge, left and right; the round badges and the fold triangles keep
    their own sizes.

## How it is kept (the coder's, within the changelist)

1. Qt counts a window as active while a dialog of its has the keyboard, so two Esc keys answered at once and neither
   acted: a dialog's keys answer only while it is the focus window (`Appearance.focusWindow`).
2. Backspace and Del are both keys of Move to Trash; a person who set other keys keeps them.
