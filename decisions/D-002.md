# D-002: C++20 with Qt 6

Approved by the owner on 21 November 2026, before any code existed.

## What was decided

**Chrono Vault is written in C++20 with Qt 6 and CMake.**

## Why

1. **Continuity.** Since 1998 the KDE Free Qt Foundation has an agreement with the Qt company: should the free edition
   of Qt stop, the foundation may release Qt under a BSD-style licence. No other toolkit considered has such a
   guarantee.
2. **One codebase for all five platforms** (D-001).
3. **The owner knows C++**, and can read and judge every line.

## What it costs

C++ is not memory-safe. In a password manager that is guarded by testing with the sanitizers and by testing the
importers with random, damaged input. The estimate put this at about 15 to 25% more work than the memory-safe
alternatives.

## What was refused

1. **Flutter with Dart**: one codebase for all five platforms, but its future depends on one company, the team behind
   it was reported to be shrinking, and the owner does not know Dart.
2. **Kotlin with Compose Multiplatform**: memory-safe and backed by two companies, but without the legal guarantee
   Qt has.
3. **Python with PySide6**: the iOS route was too new, and Python packages with C extensions did not work there yet.
4. **Rust**: memory-safe, but its window toolkits were thin on phones, and it is unlike any language the owner knows.
