# D-078: Another vault opens without anything in between

Approved by the owner on 26 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.7.110).

**The owner's rule: never show a window or page that needs no interaction.** The slow making of a key from a password
(about a second, by design: it protects the vault file against guessing on another computer) stays, on the condition that
nothing flashes.

1. **A vault that opens without anything typed** (the primary with its kept key, or a vault whose password is kept in the
   primary) is opened while the vault you are in stays on screen; then the two swap in one step, with no page between.
   For the left column, Settings > Vaults' Open, the "Open a vault" list and the file picker.
2. Meanwhile nothing can be changed in the vault shown: the mouse and the keys no longer reach the main window's or the
   settings window's content, and the menus that change it are grey. "Opening “…”…" is said at the bottom. A lock, or
   another vault opened, comes first and ends the opening.
3. **If it fails**, the vault shown stays open and the reason is said, with the vault's name (in Settings too). A kept
   password that no longer opens its vault is not used again while the primary still holds it, so the vault's own master
   password is asked the next time.
4. On the start-up page nothing changes: a Closed vault picked there opens from that page.
5. Not done (struck by the owner): keeping the keys made for the rest of the session.

`VaultController::startSwitch` in `src/ui/vault_controller.cpp`; the shields `switchingShield` in `qml/Main.qml` and
`settingsSwitchingShield` in `qml/SettingsWindow.qml`.
