# D-031: the keyfile, and changing the master password

**Taken in autonomous mode** (G-26) on 22 November 2026, within the owner's changelist for `ROADMAP.md` steps 6 to 9
(Q22 taken as recommended).

## What was decided

1. **A vault may be locked with a keyfile beside its master password** (`REQUIREMENTS.md` 4.2): any file the person
   chooses (64 MB at most), or a new one of 64 random bytes made by Chrono Vault (`.chronovaultkey`).
2. **How**: Argon2id is given the password followed by the keyfile's SHA-256. Such a vault's header is format 2 with
   key derivation 2 (D-014); a vault without a keyfile stays format 1, so an older Chrono Vault still opens it, and
   says "made by a newer Chrono Vault" of one with a keyfile.
3. **Losing the keyfile loses the vault** (OR-15.5): wherever a keyfile is chosen or made, the window says so in bold
   and asks the person to keep a copy apart from the vault.
4. **The keyfile's place is remembered per vault** on this computer, never inside the vault. The unlock page shows it,
   and asks for it when a vault needs one that is not known here.
5. **File > Change master password or keyfile…**: the current master password, the new one twice, and a keyfile or
   none. The vault is written with the new key as safely as every save (OR-15); nothing changes when the current
   password is wrong, the keyfile cannot be read, or the disk cannot be written.

**Since 0.6.103 (D-073): the keyfile can be chosen in the standard dialog too.**
