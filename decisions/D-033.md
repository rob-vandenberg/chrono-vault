# D-033: an item as the reference program has it

**Taken in autonomous mode** (G-26) on 22 November 2026, within the owner's changelist for importing the reference
program's export. The owner's rule: import exactly as the reference program would, identical in every way; what
Chrono Vault lacks for that is built first.

## What was decided

1. **Sections**: a section is a field of its own kind holding a heading. In the item's view every section is its own
   card, its heading in bold grey at the value column; the fields before the first section stand in a card without a
   heading; a section with no field to show is left out.
2. **The reference program's field types**: text, password, username, email, url, phone, date, numeric, one-time
   code, several lines, and the card types expiry, security code, PIN and card type. Dates are kept as the text they
   are. An email address and a web address are shown as links, without underline.
3. **A hide mark on any field** (sensitive): shown as eight dots while Settings > Security "Hide sensitive fields" is
   on (the default), with Show and Hide in the row's "…" menu.
4. **Hidden fields** (the reference program's types that start with a dot) and **deleted fields** are kept with the
   item and carried through every save, never shown. Empty fields are not shown in the item's view either.
5. **Field history**: every change of a field's value keeps the earlier value with the time it was set; "…" >
   History shows them, newest first, hidden ones as dots until Show. Every field has an id within its item; fields of
   an older vault get theirs when it is read.
6. **An item's own subtitle and note**, apart from its fields; the subtitle is the list's second line when set, and
   Settings > Customize "Show subtitles" (on by default) shows or hides that line. The note is shown in a card of its
   own below the fields.
7. **Times**: "Last Modified:" and "Created:" below the cards, as the long date with the weekday and the time with
   seconds; Autosubmit Login is kept and shown as a tick box under a login, though nothing acts on it (D-006).
8. **Trash and Archive**: Move to Trash in place of Delete; Archive and Trash under "Others" in the left column; every
   other view leaves their items out; Restore, Unarchive, and Delete Permanently (after a question) in the Trash.
9. **The look**: the item's header (icon, bold title, pencil, star, "…") with a thin line under it; cards a little
   lighter than the page with a faint border and rounded corners, rows about 54 pixels high with a faint line
   between, the row under the mouse lightly filled; a field of several lines with its value at the top; the one-time
   code's ring red in its last five seconds; the chosen item in the list with a grey fill and a blue bar.
10. **The contents format 4** holds all of this; formats 1 to 3 are still read.

**Since 0.5.82 (D-062) Move to Trash asks first.**
