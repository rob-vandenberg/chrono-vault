# D-069: Exact UI scaling

Approved by the owner on 25 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.6.92; way A of two he was shown).

1. **Everything is its size at 100% times the UI scaling, exactly.** Before, texts and gaps were rounded one by one, which
   added up: at 110% General came out 5% too large and needed a scroll bar. The settings window's size stays (D-066).
2. **A text is drawn at its size at 100% and enlarged**, because Qt draws a font only in whole pixels (110% and 115% were
   the same text). Texts therefore break at the same words at every scaling, where their room scales along.
3. **A typed text too** (text fields, text areas, number boxes): a frame of exact size with the enlarged text in it.
4. **Sizes keep their exact value; only what is drawn goes to whole pixels**: icons, ticks, lines and borders.
5. **At 100% the windows look as before.**

## How it is kept

1. `style/ScaledText.qml` (Label and every control's text), `style/IconText.qml` (icon beside text), `style/TextField.qml`,
   `style/TextArea.qml`, `style/SpinBox.qml`.
2. Qt's row and column layouts round every item up; `style/ExactRow.qml` and `style/ExactColumn.qml` take the sum of it out
   of the room between the items ("gap"). Grid layouts are Qt's own.
3. `Appearance.font` is the font at 100%; the program's own font stays the scaled one, for the file dialogs.
4. A text that breaks takes its new room after the layout that gave it, never in the middle of it.
