# D-082: One design for every dialog

Approved by the owner on 27 November 2026, first through the Proceed in autonomous mode on the changelist of version
0.8.120, then rewritten to his design concept and his approved mockups through the Proceed on the changelist of
version 0.8.121 (Q18 to Q22 of that brainstorm). The mockups are the target: `scratchpad/dialogs and mockups/`, their
source `scratchpad/design/review/index-source.html` (private, not in git). His model is "Open a vault".

**The concept**: space shows what belongs together (close inside a group, clearly more between groups); one left edge,
the right side mirroring it; a calm frame, the same space all round; a few sizes of space, each with one meaning;
hierarchy by size and weight; content first, no dead space, nothing floating; the eye's path ends at the action.

1. **The frame**: 16 pixels from the dialog's sides, top and bottom at 100%, times the UI scaling; 16 between the
   content and the buttons. Inside the content: 8 within a group (a text and its field, a label and its field, the
   paragraphs of one text), 16 between groups, 24 between sections.
2. **Buttons go in groups by meaning**, 8 pixels apart inside a group and alike in width only inside it: the decision
   (OK, Cancel and the dialog's own buttons beside them) at the right; a group about the content at the left (Skip this
   update, Show, Delete Field, Add Custom Icon, the three ways to settle a conflict); a second group at the left starts
   after `DialogWindow.ButtonGap`. Settings: Create vault and Open vault, Restore defaults its own group, OK and Cancel.
3. **Sizes**: buttons 28 pixels high and at least 88 wide; input fields and choices 28 high, as the buttons (the owner:
   "it makes it look less cramped"). The style reads `dialogDesign` of the window, so the main window keeps its own.
4. **Discard apart at the far left** in every question that has it, in red; Enter and Esc never reach it.
5. **Red**: every button with red text uses #ff6b6b (Delete Field, Discard). Red error lines are unchanged.
6. **Fixed or resizable**: a window is resizable when it holds content whose size comes from outside (a list of vaults,
   a table, a licence text, a file list, history values, an icon grid, a list of fields): Open a vault, Settings, Sync
   conflicts, Sync conflict, the file dialogs, Choose Icon, the template editor, About and a field's history. It opens
   as tall as its content, up to a limit, and remembers its size under a name of its own. Every other dialog is fixed,
   440 wide and exactly as tall as its content; texts wrap. The storage tiles count as fixed, so Create a vault, Move
   a vault and Import are fixed.
7. **Where extra room goes**: the part with the changing content takes all of it; everything else keeps its natural
   size and stays against its edge; inside the growing part content starts at the top left. A resizable window without
   such a part keeps its content at the top, never spread out.
8. **The WebDAV window** has its labels at the left of the fields, in one column; it is 580 wide, so a normal address
   fits without scrolling, and the person may make it wider (never taller): its fields grow.
9. **Google Drive**: the sign-in window ("Sign in with Google opens your web browser…") is shown when sign-in is
   required. Once signed in it closes and only the Google Drive file dialog is on screen, over the main window. A
   choice there is carried out with the file dialog still open; a failure is shown in its own error line; its Cancel
   ends it all. The line "Only vaults created or moved here by Chrono Vault are shown…" (Q15) stands in that file
   dialog. **WebDAV** goes straight to its file dialog once connected, its window staying where server, user and
   password are typed.
10. **The key in use**: "This key has already been assigned" in bold as first line, no "?" picture (Q22).
11. **Yes and No** show their words without the "&"; the translator's letters still press them (D-079). A "&" given
    to a button while it is being made left it without any text at all.
12. **Sync conflict**: Enter applies once Apply can be used and the keyboard is on no button.
13. **Texts**: no text is bold as a whole; every password field shows its placeholder in the normal font and the
    password in the monospaced one.
14. **One window used for two dialogs** (Open a vault and Create a vault) keeps each its own size.

Tests: `test_dialog_design` and `test_dialog_design_200` (every dialog: 16 edge, 8 inside a button group and alike
there, buttons and fields 28 high, buttons at least 88 wide, fixed ones fixed to their content and 440 wide, WebDAV 580
and wider by hand, Discard at the left, Yes and No show their words, a resizable one made taller keeps its content at
the top and its buttons at the bottom), `test_webdav_windows` (only the Google Drive file dialog after sign-in),
`test_intuitive` and `test_intuitive_bars` (Yes and its letter), `test_audit_windows`.
