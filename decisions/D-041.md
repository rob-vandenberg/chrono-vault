# D-041: waits after wrong master passwords

Approved by the owner on 22 November 2026 (changelist items 28 to 31, Proceed; Q57 and Q58 answered yes); the details
below taken in autonomous mode (G-26) are marked so.

## What was decided

1. Attempts 1 to 3 at once; 4 and 5 after 10 seconds each; 6 to 10 after 1 minute each; from 11 on, 10 minutes before
   each. Before attempts 26, 51, 76 and so on, one wait of 1 hour in place of the 10 minutes.
2. The count is kept per vault in the settings, so a restart does not end it; a correct unlock sets it back to zero;
   a wait still running goes on after a restart.
3. During a wait the unlock page greys the password field and Unlock, with "Try again in 0:52" counting down.
4. The PIN, when it comes (0.0.27), gets the same rule in a count of its own.

## Taken in autonomous mode

1. Only a typed master password that does not open the vault counts; a password saved in the primary that no longer
   fits is told apart, as before, and does not count.
2. Any correct unlock (typed, by finger, or with the password saved in the primary) sets the count back to zero.
3. A clock set back never makes a wait longer than the wait itself.
4. The fingerprint stays usable during a wait.
5. The settings know the vault only by a fingerprint of its path, not by its name.
