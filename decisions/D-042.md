# D-042: the collapsible sidebar, and the fingerprint reader held only while asked

Approved by the owner on 22 November 2026 (changelist items 1 to 25, Proceed).

## What was decided

1. Settings > General: Start at startup, Start minimized, then Keep Chrono Vault running in the system tray.
2. The sidebar collapses to its icons and expands again with a button at its top left (the owner's mockups):
   collapsed it shows the vault's icon with its ▾, Home, Favorites, each group's ▾ and the entries' icons, at a
   narrow fixed width, their names on hover; expanding gives back the earlier width; remembered for the next start.
   View > Hide Sidebar stays apart and hides it whole.
3. Each group (Categories, Tags, Others) has a ▾ beside its heading in both states; it folds and unfolds the group
   (the owner); remembered.
4. Archive and Trash have icons of their own, a box and a bin.
5. The splitters between the columns react to the mouse over 4 pixels instead of 2; the line shown stays thin.
6. **The fingerprint reader is never held hostage, and there is no time limit (the owner):** Chrono Vault holds it
   while a finger is being asked, and gives it back the moment that is settled: the master password typed, Cancel, the
   finger recognized, the window closed into the tray, another vault chosen, the program quitting. A finger not
   recognized leaves the question open: the reader stays held and asks again.
7. Settings > Security > Unlock with fingerprint is a switch; switched on, it asks for one touch to make sure it works
   ("Touch the fingerprint reader…" once the reader is ready), with a Cancel; the outcome is told there. While it waits
   the switch cannot be pressed again; a Cancel is no failure.
8. The unlock page asks for the finger whenever it is on screen for a vault with the fingerprint on: at a start once
   the window shows, after a lock, for another vault, back from the tray; never while the window is hidden.
9. A message that starts with the name "fingerprint" starts with a capital letter.

## Found on the owner's laptop (Kubuntu)

Turning the fingerprint on asked for a finger without saying so, so nobody touched the reader and the check kept it;
pressing Turn on again ended that check and reported it as a failure, and `fprintd-verify` was refused. The unlock page
asked for the finger only when it turned visible, never at a start.

## Changed in 0.2.47 (the owner, 23 November 2026)

The expanded sidebar is at least 180 pixels wide. Dragging its splitter, it holds at 180 (or, collapsed, at its icons)
while the pointer goes on; when the pointer passes 120 pixels from the window's left edge it snaps shut (or open at
180), exactly as the collapse button does. All times the UI scaling.

## Changed in 0.2.48 (the owner, 23 November 2026)

The dead zone is 100 pixels each way: the expanded sidebar snaps shut when the pointer passes 80 (180 less 100), the
collapsed one opens when it passes 164 (64 plus 100). All times the UI scaling.

## Changed in 0.2.49 (the owner, 23 November 2026)

The dead zone is 80 pixels each way: shut past 100, open past 144. All times the UI scaling.

**Since 0.6.103 (D-073): the finger is asked in the standard dialog too.**
