# D-022: the settings window's sidebar and its OK, Cancel and Restore defaults

Approved by the owner on 22 November 2026 (changelist of version 0.0.6), after the owner's picture of Chrono
Viewer's settings window.

## What was decided

1. **The sidebar** is a framed panel on the left with a darker background, the topics as plain names without icons,
   the chosen topic marked in the highlight colour across the panel's whole width. The panel is as wide as the
   longest topic name and runs down to the row of buttons. Its colours are the palette's (base, mid, highlight).
2. **Three buttons at the bottom right**, in this order: **Restore defaults**, **OK** (with a check mark, the default
   button) and **Cancel** (with a crossed circle). The two icons are drawn for Chrono Vault in `art/`.
3. **They work as in Chrono Viewer** (the owner's Proceed on the recommendation): OK keeps the changes of every topic,
   whichever topic is shown, and Enter does the same (Enter on a button with the keyboard focus presses that button, OR-16; each opening starts with the focus on the topics); Cancel, Esc or closing the window throws them away; Restore defaults puts back the
   topic being shown and no other, and OK still has to keep it. Opened again, the window shows the settings as they
   are. Restore defaults leaves a setting the window shows greyed (the tray setting on a desktop without a tray).
4. **The defaults** are named once in the program, where the settings are read: lock after 5 minutes, clear the
   clipboard after 30 seconds, keep running in the tray, not started at startup, not started minimized, check for
   updates daily.
5. **Start at startup is written outside Chrono Vault** and can fail: then OK keeps the other settings, the window
   stays open on General, shows the setting as it really is, and says so beside the buttons.

**Since 0.5.82 (D-062) the Vaults page has no Restore defaults.**
