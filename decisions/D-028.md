# D-028: tags and favourites

**Taken in autonomous mode** (G-26) on 22 November 2026, within the owner's changelist for `ROADMAP.md` steps 6 to 9
(Q20 taken as recommended).

## What was decided

1. **An item carries any number of tags**, added in the item editor (typed, then Enter; a tag still being typed is
   kept when the item is saved) and removed there.
2. **A slash nests a tag**: "Work/Servers" is "Servers" under "Work". Spaces around each part and empty parts are
   left out, so " Work / / Servers " is kept as "Work/Servers".
3. **The left column shows the tags as a tree**; choosing one narrows the list to the items with that tag or a tag
   nested under it. Its right-click menu renames the tag, and every tag under it, in every item, or takes it off
   every item. The items themselves stay.
4. **The star in the item's view marks a favourite** (ref); "Favorites" in the left column narrows the list to them.
5. **The search also finds an email address and a tag.**
