# D-077: Open a vault

Approved by the owner on 26 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.7.108), from his mockup.

1. **Opening a vault** shows "Open a vault": "Your vaults" on top, a row per known vault as in Settings > Vaults (name, the
   primary's crown, the padlock, where it is kept, when a WebDAV vault was last synchronized; no menu), and below it
   "Storage locations", one row of tiles: This Device and WebDAV (others once they work). OK and Cancel.
2. The open vault is not listed; a vault not found is struck through, "Not found", and cannot be chosen.
3. One click selects a row; OK, a double click or Enter opens it: a Closed vault at once, a Locked one in the standard dialog
   (with "Remember my credentials" while logged in with the primary). A tile goes on at once.
4. The storage locations keep their height; a taller window gives the room to the list, which scrolls.
5. From File > Open vault, Settings > Vaults' Open vault, and the start page; from the start-up page's own "Open another
   vault…" without the list, which that page has already. Creating and moving keep "Select Storage" as it was.
6. **The primary's crown** in the vault list of the start-up page and of the standard dialog, and in its field.
