# D-074: Another primary vault; Remember my credentials; Open, Closed, Locked

Approved by the owner on 25 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.6.105).

1. **"Make primary vault"** in a vault's menu (Settings > Vaults), after a short confirmation: only the crown moves, and that
   is remembered on this computer. No passwords are moved or removed: the old primary keeps what it holds, and uses it again
   when it is made the primary again.
2. **"Remember my credentials"** in the standard dialog, in Settings > Vaults: not for the primary; on by default; kept in the
   primary once the typed password was right; a tick taken away is remembered for that vault. Gray while the primary is
   locked: "Your primary vault is locked. Open it first." "Create a new vault" has the same words.
3. **The mental model (the owner)**: Open = you are in it; Closed = it opens without a password, as a door that is only
   closed; Locked = it asks one. Going to another vault closes the one you leave; Lock and autolock lock every vault, the
   kept passwords too. Inside, the primary has its items and a store of other vaults' passwords; leaving it closes the items
   only, locking it closes both.
4. **The padlocks, drawn by the owner**: `art/padlock-open.svg` green, `art/padlock-closed.svg` and `art/padlock-locked.svg` (filled)
   gray, in Settings > Vaults and in the dialog's list.

**Since 0.6.106 (D-075): Close and Lock per vault; the tick box on the start-up page too.**

**Since 0.8.119: the top bar's spaces alike (the owner).** The space from the right edge of the Generate Password
button to the padlock equals the space from the padlock to the gear, both measured between what is seen: the padlock
fills 4 to 20 of its 24, the gear from 2.4 (3.3 less half its 1.8 line), each picture 24 by 24 in a circle of 32. The
padlock's button has a left margin of the gear's empty edge in its circle; `test_intuitive_bars` measures both spaces
on the screen at 100% and 200%.
