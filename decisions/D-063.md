# D-063: Selected and focused; the Item menu; the empty list; the vault's counts

Approved by the owner on 24 November 2026 (Proceed, autonomous mode, on the changelist of version 0.5.83). Changes
D-062 point 3, D-062's search (point 4) and D-033's and D-039's Item menu and marks.

## What was decided

1. **Selected** items are what an action applies to; **the focused item** is the one shown at the right. Selecting never
   changes the right side.
2. A click selects one item and focuses it. Shift+click and Shift with Up, Down, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End select
   from the focused item; Ctrl+click adds or takes away; Ctrl+A selects all: **none of these moves the focus**, knowingly
   unlike Windows Explorer. A plain key goes one step from the focused item and selects that one alone.
3. **The marks**, in the list and the sidebar: no blue background, one gray for everything selected, a thin blue bar at
   the focused line (one in each).
4. An item not in the newly chosen list is neither selected nor shown.
5. **The Item menu always acts on the selected items**, never on an entry of the sidebar: Add to / Remove from Favorites,
   Archive / Unarchive, Change Category, Move to Trash, Restore, Delete Permanently…. The copy entries act on the focused
   item. Delete is Move to Trash's key and does the same wherever the keyboard is. The right-click menu holds the same,
   only what is possible.
6. **Change Category** changes the category alone; fields and template stay. It and Favorites ask nothing.
7. **Settings > Customize** is the other place for the sidebar's right-click; it gets the tags. Not Archive and Trash.
8. **An empty list** shows our own drawing of an open, empty vault and why it is empty; a search that finds nothing only
   says so.
9. **With no item focused** the right side shows the vault's name and clickable counts; the audit comes above them later.

## How it is kept (the coder's, taken in autonomous mode)

1. Ctrl+click or Shift+click with no item focused is a plain click.
2. Change Category offers the categories the left column shows, as New Item does.
3. The buttons at the right act on the item shown there, also when others are selected.
4. In the Trash, Move to Trash's keys ask about deleting for good, as before (D-062).
5. An action that takes the focused item out of the list focuses the next one; other selected items stay selected. When
   the search or a newly chosen list leaves the focused item out while others are selected, the first of those is focused.
6. An item that leaves the list otherwise (its star taken off under Favorites; a new one stored in another category) stays
   shown, and is not selected until it is listed again.
7. Under Favorites, a tag, the Archive and the Trash the empty list has one line; an empty Home reads "No items yet".
