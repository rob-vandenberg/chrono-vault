# D-009: the order of the platforms

Approved by the owner on 21 November 2026, before any code existed.

## What was decided

1. **Linux and Windows are developed first.** Windows 10 and newer.
2. **Android, macOS and iOS come later**, weeks or months from the start, when their needs are dealt with: macOS and
   iOS can only be built and signed on a Mac with Xcode.
3. **All five stay the goal** (D-001): every design decision keeps them in mind, so nothing built for Linux and Windows
   has to be undone for the others.
