# D-071: Padlocks and Open in Settings > Vaults; round picture buttons

Approved by the owner on 25 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.6.100; the padlocks as in his mockup).

1. **Settings > Vaults**: after a vault's name (and its crown) a padlock: closed and grey "Locked", open and green "Open", its
   shackle swung to the right. None for a vault not found.
2. **A vault's menu begins with Open or Lock, by its state.** Open opens at once with the password kept in the primary, or
   the primary with its key kept from this session; else its master password is asked. One vault is open at a time. An item
   being edited is asked about first. A right click on the row opens the same menu.
3. **A button that is only a picture or a sign** (`qml/PictureButton.qml`): 32 by 32 times the UI scaling, no frame, a gray
   circle under the mouse, darker when pressed or while its menu is open, a blue edge with the keyboard.
4. **A menu's own button is the vertical dots** everywhere.
5. As they were: the eye in password fields, the fold triangles, the small signs of Keyboard shortcuts, the sort order, the
   pencil badges.

**Since 0.6.102 (the owner): when Open uses a kept password that no longer opens the vault, Settings says so too.**
