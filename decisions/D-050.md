# D-050: The icon picker

Approved by the owner on 22 November 2026 (Proceed, version 0.0.40).

1. The item editor's top row: "Add ▾" at the left; Save, then Cancel, at the right; 12 pixels between the buttons
   and the icon below. A click anywhere on the item's icon opens the icon picker; the old menu ("Choose icon…",
   "Remove icon") is gone.
2. "Choose Icon", after the reference program's picker, without its Search field: "Custom Icons" first, starting
   with "Default icon" (the item's category's icon), then one section per folder named in `icons.md`. The chosen icon
   carries a blue circle with a check mark; OK is greyed until an icon is chosen; Cancel; "Add Custom Icon" at the
   bottom left adds a picture (JPG, PNG, BMP, ICO, SVG) to the vault's own icons and chooses it; a right-click on one
   of the vault's own offers "Delete icon", asked first when items carry it, which then get their category's icon.
3. The vault's own icons are kept inside the vault (contents format 5), so the same vault shows them on every device
   (the owner). An item keeps its icon as its own PNG, also when it came from a shipped icon.
4. The icons that ship: the project's `icons/` folder, `icons.md` with one line per section ("folder: Heading", in
   the order shown; empty lines and lines without a colon skipped; other folders ignored) and the folders `web`,
   `creditcard`, `miscellaneous`. It is installed beside the program (`/opt/chrono-vault/icons`,
   `C:\Program Files\chrono-vault\icons`) and copied at each start into the person's own folder
   (`~/.local/share/chrono-vault/icons`, `%LOCALAPPDATA%\chrono-vault\icons`, Local and not Roaming as Microsoft
   advises for larger files); nothing already there is written over, so a person's changes stay. The picker reads the
   person's folder.
5. The headings go through the translation (context "IconSections"): `update_translations` takes them from
   `icons/icons.md`; a heading without a translation is shown as written.
6. Settings > Security: the master password reminder stands just below "Change the Master Password".
