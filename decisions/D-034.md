# D-034: the editor as the reference program's

**Taken in autonomous mode** (G-26) on 22 November 2026, within the owner's changelist for importing the reference
program's export (points 18 and 31 to 36), from the owner's pictures of the reference program's editor.

## What was decided

1. **At the top**: Cancel at the left, which leaves; with changes not saved yet it first asks "Save your changes?"
   (Save, Discard, Cancel), so one mis-click never loses an edit (OR-15); at the right "Add ▾" (Attachment, Section,
   Field) and a blue Save.
2. **The icon** with a small pencil badge at its lower right, which offers Choose icon… and Remove icon; **the
   title** in one text box across the width; a thin line under them.
3. **One row per field**: the label right-aligned in grey, the value in a text box (a hidden field with dots and the
   eye inside, a field of several lines as a text area, a one-time code in a monospaced font with "Enter secret
   key"). The row under the mouse is lightly filled and shows the drag handle (≡) at its left, to drag the field up or
   down, and a blue "…" at its right. Empty fields are shown here; deleted and hidden fields are not.
4. **A section** shows as its heading, bold, at the value column.
5. **"…" opens Field Options**: Field Name (for a field of the person's own; a built-in field keeps its name), Field
   Type (the reference program's types), the Sensitive switch, Delete Field in red at the bottom left, Save at the
   bottom right. A section has only its name. A field added through "Add ▾" whose Field Options are closed without
   Save is not added.
6. **Generate** beside a password field opens the password generator, as before.
7. **On Windows a save is tried again briefly** when another program holds the vault file at the moment it is put in
   place: at most ten times within about a second, only for "access denied" or "in use" (the owner, Q37).

## Changed in 0.3.62 (the owner)

Save stores the item and closes the editor on the saved item; before, the editor stayed open after Save.
