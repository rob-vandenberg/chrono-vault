# D-075: Close and Lock per vault

Approved by the owner on 25 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.6.106).

1. **A vault's menu in Settings > Vaults, by its state**: Open with a kept password, or the primary: Close. Open without
   one: Lock. Closed: Open and Lock. Locked: Open. Lock never works on more than the one vault it is chosen for.
2. **Close** closes only the open vault; the key store stays open. An edit is asked about first; a save is waited for.
3. **Lock on a Closed vault** deletes its password from the primary (the item stays, its password field empty), without a
   confirmation; the next time its own password is asked. When that cannot be saved, Settings says so and it stays Closed.
4. **Lock on an open vault without a kept password** locks that one. **Lock on the Closed primary** locks its key store;
   nothing is deleted and an open vault stays open.
5. **The start-up page begins with the primary** at program start, after Close, and after 4's first Lock while the key store
   is open; when it is not found, with the vault used last. Entering its password opens the primary. Another vault picked is
   opened with its own password; the primary then stays locked and gives no help.
6. **After a lock (Ctrl+L, the lock button, the tray, autolock)** the way in is the way back: a vault opened with its kept
   password asks the primary's password ("Enter the Master Password of “Primary” to return to “A”") and opens by itself, the
   primary's items never shown; a vault whose password was typed while the primary was skipped, or is not kept, is shown
   itself and asks its own. A password typed and remembered counts as kept.
7. **A Closed vault on the start-up page** has an Open button in the field's place; another Closed one picked opens at once.
8. **"Remember my credentials" on the start-up page**: only for a Locked vault that is not the primary, while the key store
   is open.
9. **The locks of 6 also lock the key store when no vault is open.** No lock of everything deletes a kept password.

## How it is kept (the coder's, within the changelist)

1. The kept password is emptied in every item of the primary that names the vault, off the window's thread, written as every
   save (OR-15). An open primary changed elsewhere a moment ago is not written over: it is said, and tried again.
2. A change kept through a lock stays kept; picking its own vault never asks to give it up.
3. With Open in the field's place, the heading, the reminder, the keyfile line and the finger's button are hidden.

**Since 0.7.107 (D-076): point 6 became the screen lock; the vault that was open comes back, also when its password is not kept.**
