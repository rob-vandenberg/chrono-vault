# D-017: Chrono Vault's own look

Approved by the owner on 21 November 2026 (changelist of version 0.0.4).

## What was decided

1. **Chrono Vault sets its own style, Qt's Fusion**, the same on every platform, through one function the program and
   its tests both use, so the tests check what people see. On KDE Plasma the desktop's own Breeze style for Qt Quick
   failed in the editor (`ButtonBackground.qml`, `TextArea.qml`); with Fusion it does not.
2. **The fonts are Google's Noto, carried inside the program**: Noto Sans for all text, at the system's normal size,
   and Noto Sans Mono for passwords and one-time codes. They are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; their licence,
   `fonts/OFL.txt`, ships in every package and beside the program. The owner made an exception to OR-2 for them.
3. **The version stands in every window title**: "Chrono Vault 0.0.4", and "<vault name> – Chrono Vault 0.0.4" with a
   vault open.
