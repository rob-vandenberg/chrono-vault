# D-072: The width of menus, and when they close

Approved by the owner on 25 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.6.101).

1. **A menu and a submenu are as wide as their longest entry**, with its margins, shortcut, arrow and check mark, and at least
   120 pixels times the UI scaling (measured by the owner). The list of a choice box is as wide as its box.
2. **Menus, submenus and the lists of choice boxes close when their window is no longer the one with the keyboard**: a click
   in another program or on the desktop, Alt+Tab, another window of ours. Qt draws them inside their window, where they hear
   only of clicks in it.
