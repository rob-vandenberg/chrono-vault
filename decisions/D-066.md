# D-066: The topics of Settings

Approved by the owner on 25 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.6.88).

1. The topics, in this order: **General, Security, Vaults, Categories, Tags, Templates, Keyboard shortcuts**. Customize
   and Clipboard are gone.
2. **Categories, Tags and Templates** each are a topic, with the lists and buttons Customize had (D-027, D-056, D-063).
   They belong to the open vault and are kept in it at once, so they have no Restore defaults, as Vaults (D-062).
3. **General** has what was left: Show subtitles in the item list and Use website icons (D-033, D-036), and Clear the
   clipboard after … seconds (D-049), with General's Restore defaults.
4. The right column's cards Categories and Tags open their own topic (D-063).
5. The window stays 730 by 650 (D-023), and General fits it without a scroll bar.
