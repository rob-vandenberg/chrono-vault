# D-015: what a vault holds

**Taken in autonomous mode** (G-26) on 21 November 2026, within the owner's changelist for the first version.

## What was decided

1. **A vault holds items.** Each item has an id, a template, a title, the moments it was made and last changed, and a
   list of fields.
2. **Two templates in the first version**: Login (user name, password, web address, one-time code, notes) and Secure
   note (notes). The person can add custom fields of their own to any item, each with a name of their choosing.
3. **A built-in field is known by its role**, not its name, so its name is shown in the person's own language; a
   custom field keeps the name the person gave it.
4. **The contents are stored as JSON inside the sealed vault**, with a format number of their own. Contents of the
   wrong shape are refused as a whole, never partly read, and a newer format is refused, so a vault is never written
   back without what a newer version put in it (OR-15).

## Changed on 22 November 2026 (version 0.0.14, autonomous mode)

The two templates became thirteen in eight categories, with the person's own categories and templates kept in the
vault (D-027); items also carry tags and a favourite mark (D-028) and attachments (D-029). The contents format is
now 3; formats 1 and 2 are still read.

## Changed on 22 November 2026 (version 0.0.17, autonomous mode)

Contents format 4: sections, the reference program's field types, hide marks, hidden and deleted fields, field
history and ids, an item's own subtitle and note, trash, archive and autosubmit (D-033).
