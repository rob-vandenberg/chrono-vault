# D-046: no bounce, and the About box's text read by keys

Approved by the owner on 22 November 2026 (Proceed).

1. Nothing bounces: every scrolling page and list stops at its ends (`Flickable.StopAtBounds`); the style's own ScrollView does it for every page, the item list and the settings topics have it themselves.
2. The About box's licence text has a scroll bar that is always there and clearly seen in light and dark, with a handle at least a twelfth of its length.
3. It scrolls with Up/Down (a line), Page Up/Down (a page) and Home/End (start and end), and takes the keyboard when the box opens.
