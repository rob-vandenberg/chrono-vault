# D-046: no bounce, and the About box's text read by keys

Approved by the owner on 22 November 2026 (Proceed).

1. Nothing bounces: every scrolling page and list stops at its ends (`Flickable.StopAtBounds`); the style's own ScrollView does it for every page, the style's Menu and ComboBox for the lists Qt builds for menus and choice boxes, and every ListView, Flickable and GridView of ours has it itself. Since 0.7.112 the rule is held by `tests/unit/test_no_bounce.cpp`, which reads every QML file in `qml/` and `style/` and fails on a list without it (it came back once, in the vault dropdown, because the rule was kept per list).
2. The About box's licence text has a scroll bar that is always there and clearly seen in light and dark, with a handle at least a twelfth of its length.
3. It scrolls with Up/Down (a line), Page Up/Down (a page) and Home/End (start and end), and takes the keyboard when the box opens.
