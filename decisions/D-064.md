# D-064: The tray icon

Approved by the owner on 25 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.5.85). Changes the tray's icon of D-019.

## What was decided

1. The system tray shows a plain vault, drawn by the owner; everything else keeps the program's blue icon.
2. On a tray that is surely dark: the white vault (`art/chrono-vault-tray.svg`). In every other case, also when nothing
   is known: the same vault 7% smaller with a thin dark edge (`art/chrono-vault-tray-light.svg`), seen on both.
3. Whether it is dark: on Windows the taskbar's own setting (`SystemUsesLightTheme`), elsewhere the desktop's light or
   dark. Chrono Vault's own theme has no part in it. A change is followed at once.

## What it cannot see

A Linux panel with a colour of its own. A dark panel on a light desktop shows the edged vault, which is fine; a light
panel on a dark desktop shows the white one.
