# D-079: Question dialogs, their keys, and where a vault is kept

Approved by the owner on 26 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.7.111; Q278 to Q289).

1. **A dialog that only asks** (a text and buttons) opens exactly as tall as its text, 400 times the scaling wide (the
   text wraps), cannot be resized and remembers no size: Move to the Trash and the other item questions, Delete
   permanently, Delete icon, A file is being read, Save your changes?, A change is not saved (both), Trust this
   certificate?, Make primary vault, Remove PIN, Move the vault, Forget the vault, Disconnect from WebDAV, Export the
   vault, Update downloaded, Chrono Vault was updated. Every other dialog stays resizable.
2. **Enter presses the selected button, and only that one.** The selected button is the one holding the keyboard, and
   it always shows the blue edge. When a dialog opens, the first selected is OK or Yes, or the safe one (No, Cancel)
   where the action cannot be undone. Tab and the Left and Right arrows move the selection. A text or password field
   keeps Enter as its own "done".
3. **Yes/No without a field to type in:** Y presses Yes and N presses No, without Alt; the letters are underlined and
   are the translator's own ("&Yes", "&No").
4. **Esc presses Cancel**, or Close when the dialog has no Cancel and no field to type in; otherwise nothing. The title
   bar × stays everywhere and means no choice: Cancel, or No in a Yes/No dialog. Changes D-052.
5. **The owner's rule for questions:** a question is asked in plain positive words, never as a double negative, with
   the buttons that answer it (OK/Cancel, Yes/No, Abort/Retry/Ignore …).
6. **Settings > Vaults and Open a vault** show at the left of each vault where it is kept: the screen
   (`art/storage-device.svg`) for this computer, the cloud (`art/storage-webdav.svg`) for WebDAV. pCloud, Google Drive,
   Dropbox and OneDrive get their own logos once they are storage types of their own.

`qml/DialogWindow.qml` (`fitsContent`, `markSelected`, `pressEscape`, the Y and N shortcuts); the key-in-use window of
Settings > Keyboard shortcuts follows 2 and 4 by itself.
