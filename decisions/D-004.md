# D-004: the licence

Approved by the owner on 21 November 2026, before any code existed.

## What was decided

**Everything published of Chrono Vault is AGPL-3.0-only, with the owner's copyright alone in every file**, the same as
his other chrono programs.

## Why

An open-source licence lets the program use the free edition of Qt on every platform, iOS included. A closed program
would need a paid Qt licence.
