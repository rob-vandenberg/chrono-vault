# D-076: A PIN per vault; the lock as a screen lock

Approved by the owner on 26 November 2026 (Proceed on the changelist of version 0.7.107, the version his own).

1. **Each vault has its own PIN**, which opens only that vault. It is set in the vault's menu in Settings > Vaults with "Set
   PIN…" (then "Change PIN…" and "Remove PIN…"), after that vault's master password is typed; a finger or another PIN is not
   enough. At least 4 characters. Remove asks for a confirmation, no password. The PIN line left Settings > Security.
2. **The PIN is asked only after a lock** (autolock, Ctrl+L, the lock button, the tray's Lock): never at program start, never
   after Lock or Close in a vault's menu, and not in the dialog of Settings, Import and Export.
3. **A lock is a screen lock.** What is open is put away under the vault the person logged in with: the primary when its key
   store is open, otherwise the vault itself. That vault's PIN, password or finger gives everything back: the vault that was
   open, and the key store.
4. **The PIN page shows the list.** Getting into another vault there is a new login: what was put away goes, the key store
   is locked, and from then on the person is logged in through that vault (the owner's example: a manager and his staff).
5. **The link** under the PIN field reads "Unlock using password instead".
6. **The primary asks its master password, any other vault its password**: "Enter the Master Password to unlock" and "Master
   Password" for the primary, "Enter the password to unlock" and "Password" for another vault, also in the standard dialog.
7. **"Remember my credentials" only while logged in with the primary** (its key store open, not put away by a lock).
8. **The PIN of every vault of before 0.7.107 is removed** at the first start; a new one is set per vault.
9. Unchanged: a PIN works only while the program runs; a changed master password keeps it; a moved vault takes it along,
   Forget deletes it.

## How it is kept (the coder's, within the changelist)

1. On disk a vault keeps only its PIN's salt, the cost of making its key, and that key sealed with the vault's own key
   (`chrono-vault.conf`, `pins/`). A disk alone opens nothing.
2. At a lock, what was open (the open vault's key and keyfile, the primary's, the kept passwords) is packed and sealed twice
   in memory: with the key of the vault logged in with, and with its PIN's key. Nothing readable stays behind.
