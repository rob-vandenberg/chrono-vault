# D-023: the theme and the UI scaling

Approved by the owner on 22 November 2026 (changelist of version 0.0.7), as in Chrono Viewer's Settings > General.

## What was decided

1. **Theme**: Light, Dark or Follow the system, in a framed group at the top of Settings > General. Light and Dark
   are Chrono Viewer's two palettes for Qt's Fusion style, kept in `src/ui/theme.cpp`, the one place for the
   program's colours. Follow the system asks the system whether it is light or dark, and switches by itself when the
   system switches. A system that does not say counts as light.
2. **UI scaling**: one slider from 50% to 400% in steps of 5%, with a mark every 25% above it (version 0.0.11), half
   a line height of room above and below it, and the percentage to its right, below Theme. 100% is the size the desktop itself uses, so a desktop that already scales is not scaled twice.
   Changed with version 0.0.10, after the owner found 200% far too large: it scales **everything together**, as
   Chrono Mail does: text, icons, tick boxes, radio buttons, the slider's handle, paddings, spacing, margins and
   every fixed size. Chrono Vault's own Qt Quick Controls style (`ChronoVault.Style`, `style/`) gives Fusion's
   controls their sizes times the factor, with Fusion itself for every other control; Chrono Vault's windows multiply
   every size they set by the same factor.
3. **Both show at once** on every window while the settings window is open; the slider when it is let go (or at once
   when moved with the keys). OK keeps them; Cancel, Esc or closing the window puts back what is kept. Restore
   defaults on General gives Follow the system and 100%.
4. **Both are applied at every start**, before the first window shows. The first start uses Follow the system and
   100%.
5. **The settings window's pages scroll** when a page is taller than the window, as it can be at a large scale.
6. **Chrono Vault's own colours on every desktop** (version 0.0.10): a Qt Quick window takes the desktop theme's
   colours when it is made, so the light or dark look kept at the start was not drawn on a KDE desktop (the desktop's
   Breeze colours were). Every window now takes the program's colours from the start and at every later change.
   "New Item" is a flat fill in the program's blue, darker under the mouse.
7. **The settings window** opens at 730 by 650 (times the UI scaling) and is never smaller (version 0.0.11). A size a
   window remembers is never smaller than its minimum.
8. **A window larger than its screen** (version 0.0.12): the settings window's and the About box's sizes and minimums
   give way to the free room of their screen when they do not fit there.
9. **Sizes include the title bar** (version 0.0.13): 730 by 650 and 555 by 440 are the whole window as seen on the
   screen, the title bar and frame the desktop draws included; their size is taken off the inside.

**Since 0.6.89 (D-066) the settings window is 720 by 625 on its inside, without title bar and frame.**

**Since 0.6.91 (D-066): 720 by 640.**

**Since 0.6.92 (D-069): every size is exact, and a text is drawn at 100% and enlarged.**

**Since 0.6.94 (D-070): the main window keeps its size when the UI scaling changes; every other window scales along.**
