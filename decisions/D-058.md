# D-058: The unlock page with a vault selector

Approved by the owner on 23 November 2026 (Proceed, version 0.3.64), from his mockup and the coder's version of it
with a vault selector. Review rounds struck by the owner.

## What was decided

1. **The unlock page**: Chrono Vault's icon, "Welcome to Chrono Vault", "Choose your vault and enter its master
   password"; then **Vault**, a dropdown of every vault Chrono Vault knows, each with an icon for where it is kept
   (WebDAV or this device), the vault used last chosen and marked "last used"; then **Master password**, the field
   reading "Master password of <vault>", with the eye and an → button beside it; Enter unlocks too.
2. The dropdown's last entry, **"Open or create another vault…"**, goes to the start page; a change kept through a
   lock is asked about first. It replaces the "Open another vault…" button; the vault's path is no longer shown.
3. Every vault has its own master password, and the page says so: the owner found that nothing on screen told him,
   and took the one password at the start for a password of the whole program.
4. **A lock keeps the vault used last** on the unlock page (replaces D-030 point 5, where the primary came back).
   A password saved in the primary still opens its vault once the primary is unlocked.
5. **A new vault of this device** is offered in `Documents/chrono-vault` (made when asked for) until another folder
   was used in that run: where people who follow the defaults expect it (the owner, Q12).
6. **The primary vault explained** where it matters: the new vault window's tick box reads "Keep this vault's
   password in your primary vault “<name>”, so unlocking “<name>” also opens this one" (Q13).
7. The keyfile controls (D-031), the waits after wrong passwords (D-041), the reminder (D-049) and "Unlock with
   Windows Hello" / fingerprint (D-038) stay, below the field.

## Version 0.3.65 (the owner)

8. The unlock page exactly as the owner's design: his texts ("Enter the Master Password to unlock", "Master
   Password"), no labels, no → button (Enter unlocks), the icon 160, the dropdown and field 58 high, 500 wide,
   rounded; the dropdown's list as big, below the field, a line and "+" above "Open or create another vault…".
9. Only the vaults whose files are found are listed, checked in the background (OR-5) whenever the page shows. The
   vault used last, when missing, is listed struck through, greyed, "not found", and cannot be chosen; the primary
   is chosen then, or else the first vault found. A missing vault stays in the list, hidden.
10. No vault found at all: the start page, with "Your primary vault “<name>” was not found." and ↻ (look again),
    and looked for again every 5 s for a minute, every 20 s up to 10 minutes, every minute up to an hour; the vault
    used last comes back by itself when found.
